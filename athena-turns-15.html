15-Year Anniversary

Athena Turns 15

Athena Infonomics is driving innovation in policy and practice through sound research, creative data models, and fit-for-purpose digital strategies.

Institutional Origins

Some journeys begin with a question. Ours began with two:

What role can we play in generating high quality evidence to drive lasting impact? How can we achieve more with less?

That pursuit led to the birth of Athena – a name that quietly carried our intent. Athena, a symbol of wisdom. And Infonomics, the recognition that information when connected to real-world economics, policies, and people, can shape outcomes that are contextualized and customized for varying developmental challenges.

Fifteen years on, that purpose has held steady, even as our work has grown across sectors, regions, and partnerships. Whether it’s rethinking public systems, strengthening accountability, or designing pathways to inclusion, we’ve stayed close to what matters: working with our partners to bring their vision to life with the right tools, the right questions, and a deep sense of responsibility.

To turn insights into impact. To amplify that impact, together. We’re still learning. Still listening. And still working towards that goal… with intent, humility, and heart.
