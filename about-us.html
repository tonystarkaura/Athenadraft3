Milestone Trajectory

Our 15-Year Journey

Athena Infonomics was founded in Chennai, India in 2010. Chennai remains our global hub, exemplifying the local lens that we apply to our work across low- and middle-income countries. Explore the key milestones that shaped our institutional evolution.

2010 Foundation

Founding in Chennai, India

Athena Infonomics established in Chennai with a founding mission: generating high-quality evidence that bridges policy research with real-world economics to serve emerging markets.

2014 Expansion

Cross-Sectoral Development Advisory

Expanded research and advisory practice into water, sanitation, hygiene (WASH), agriculture, and urban infrastructure across South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa.

2018 Digital Platforms

Digital Public Goods & CWIS Platform

Partnered with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation to design the Citywide Inclusive Sanitation Monitoring, Learning & Evidence (CWIS-MLE) platform and geospatial toolboxes.

2021–2023 Recognition

High-Growth Recognition & TolaData Partnership

Named Economic Times Growth Champion and Financial Times High-Growth Company Asia-Pacific. Welcomed TolaData to provide web-based M&E software to international development actors.

2025–2026 AI & Federal Scale

APLYD Launch & NASA SEWP-VI Prime Award

Launched APLYD as Chief AI Officer for institutions, and awarded the prestigious NASA SEWP-VI prime acquisition contract to deliver IT and analytics solutions globally.

Software Ecosystem

About TolaData

TolaData is a specialised monitoring and evaluation platform designed to support organisations in tracking, managing, and reporting programme performance in the development sector.

Visit TolaData.com ↗

Built for non-profits and international development actors, the platform enables users to collect and manage data, track indicators against results frameworks, and visualise progress through real-time dashboards.

In partnership with Athena Infonomics, TolaData's technology is complemented by strategic expertise in research, data analytics, and digital advisory — enabling organisations to move beyond data collection towards actionable insight. Together, the collaboration strengthens data systems, enhances programme accountability, and supports more informed, evidence-based decision-making.
