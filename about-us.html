Global M&E Platform

About Toladata

TolaData is a specialised monitoring and evaluation platform designed to support organisations in tracking, managing, and reporting programme performance in the development sector.

Built for non-profits and international development actors, the platform enables users to collect and manage data, track indicators against results frameworks, and visualise progress through real-time dashboards.

In partnership with Athena Infonomics, TolaData's technology is complemented by strategic expertise in research, data analytics, and digital advisory — enabling organisations to move beyond data collection towards actionable insight.

Together, the collaboration strengthens data systems, enhances programme accountability, and supports more informed, evidence-based decision-making.
