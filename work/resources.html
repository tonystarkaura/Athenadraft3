UNICEF Rwanda Partner Early Childhood Development Machine Learning Models

Enabling Governments to Better Evaluate Early Childhood Development

In collaboration with FATE Consulting Ltd. and under the guidance of UNICEF Rwanda, we developed a number of early childhood development (ECD) resources, including an endline evaluation, a white paper, and a study on the Observation of Mother-Child Interaction (OMCI) tool. In addition, we published a new paper to share our experience in applying CART machine-learning models while exploring determinants of key ECD outcomes identified in this engagement.
