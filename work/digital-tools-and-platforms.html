Digital Tools & Platforms

Developing solutions for far-reaching impact: Athena Infonomics regularly partners with global organizations to build digital tools and platforms that provide context-specific solutions for governments, utilities, and development actors.

Gates Foundation Partner ESAWAS Aguaconsult

The EquiServe Tool

With support from the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, Athena Infonomics developed the EquiServe tool in partnership with Aguaconsult and the Eastern and Southern African Water and Sanitation Regulators Association (ESAWAS). This tool aims to help decision-makers compare and understand the outcomes of different sanitation interventions and investments. Through this web-based tool, decision-makers can consider factors that affect service delivery, ensuring they can mobilize and pool resources towards equitable sanitation solutions.

Visit EquiServe.io ↗
Platform Capabilities
  • ✓ Comparative sanitation investment modeling
  • ✓ Tariff & affordability impact calculators
  • ✓ Inclusive regulatory policy benchmark metrics
  • ✓ Evidence-backed municipal financing simulator
8 Global Cities Citywide Inclusive Sanitation M&E Platform

The CWIS-MLE Platform

The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation’s Citywide Inclusive Sanitation (CWIS) initiative provides direct support to eight cities across Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia that are redesigning their urban sanitation service systems with a CWIS approach. As part of this initiative, Athena Infonomics developed a Monitoring, Learning, and Evidence (CWIS-MLE) program to share progress, capture good practices, and serve as a resource center for cities learning about different intervention pathways.

Visit CWIScities.com ↗
Platform Capabilities
  • ✓ Cross-city performance indicator repository
  • ✓ Peer learning case studies & tools
  • ✓ Live outcome tracking across 8 global metros
  • ✓ Standardized urban sanitation benchmarks
Geospatial Analytics Fecal Sludge Management Sample Sizing

The Fecal Sludge Management (FSM) Toolbox

Working closely with the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, Athena Infonomics developed a powerful geospatial data collection, assessment, and visualization toolbox that allows users to estimate sample sizes of fecal sludge management projects, collect geo-tagged data, track survey progress, and geospatially visualize outcomes for municipal planning.

Visit FSMtoolbox.com ↗
Platform Capabilities
  • ✓ Automated sampling size calculators
  • ✓ Geotagged mobile survey data sync
  • ✓ Spatial heatmaps of containment & transport
  • ✓ Municipal infrastructure readiness scorecards

TolaData Monitoring & Evaluation Platform

A web-based monitoring and evaluation software built specifically for non-profits and international development organizations to track indicators, aggregate multi-source data, and report on real-time program outcomes across 5 continents.
